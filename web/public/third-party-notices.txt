CVSS v4.0 calculator material
-----------------------------

The CVSS v4.0 Base macro-vector score table and scoring implementation in
`web/src/lib/cvss-v4.ts` are derived from the FIRST / Red Hat reference
calculator:

- https://github.com/FIRSTdotorg/cvss-v4-calculator
- https://github.com/FIRSTdotorg/cvss-v4-calculator/blob/main/cvss_lookup.js
